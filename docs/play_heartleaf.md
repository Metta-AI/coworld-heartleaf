# Play Heartleaf

Heartleaf uses the BitWorld sprite protocol. Players connect to the
player client and control one gnome in a shared garden village.

## Goal

Collect food during the day, choose where to gather for dinner, and score
by feeding other gnomes as a host.

## Controls

- Move around the map with the directional controls.
- Press A near a garden to collect its food.
- Press A at a house entrance to go inside.
- Press A at the house exit to return to the shared map.
- Watch the clock and gather before dinner starts.

## Tips

- Hosting only scores if guests are inside your house at dinner.
- Visiting another house lets you eat without spending your own food.
- A host with more guests earns more points from the same inventory.
- Food left unused can still matter on later days.

## Local URLs

When running the game locally, open these clients:

- `http://localhost:8080/client/player`
- `http://localhost:8080/client/global`
